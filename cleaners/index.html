Before you start

A few useful answers.

Does this tell me what cleaners charge in my area?

No. The calculator uses your entered costs and margin. Check your local market and job scope separately. Examples are fictional.

Can I use it on my phone?

The tools run in a current web browser. You can download PDFs to review, print or share. Keep using the same browser for saved business records, or transfer them using a downloaded backup.

Does this send quotes or collect customer payments?

No. Download and review your quote PDF, then send it using your own email or messaging app. Customer payment collection, booking and automatic follow-ups are not included.

Is this accounting or tax software?

No. It is a pricing and job-planning tool. Set the sales-tax rate that applies to your business. Profit reports are before sales tax and income tax and depend on the costs you enter.

Do I need a VEYDRAVO finance account?

No account is required for the current business tools. They save separately from personal finance, in the browser you use.
